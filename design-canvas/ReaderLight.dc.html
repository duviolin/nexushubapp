The hidden cost of abstraction layers in modern Android runtimes
Every layer you add between the app and the metal promises safety and portability. Most of the time it delivers — until the frame budget runs out and the abstraction becomes the thing you profile against.
On Android, the runtime has spent a decade absorbing this cost quietly: ahead-of-time compilation, baseline profiles, and a garbage collector tuned for interactive latency rather than raw throughput.
The question is no longer whether to abstract, but where to place the seam — and who pays for it when the device is three years old and thermally throttled.
